The Flush Factor is the most absurd flushing game you have ever played.

You start in a grotty cubicle: a broken, dirty toilet, a rusty bucket and a bare wall. Flush, earn points and fix clogs with tools. Some things work. Other things only do something very silly. You will find out.

- Flush and build combos
- Unclog the toilet before it overflows
- Discover which tools work and which are pure chaos
- Repair and scrub your toilet, the walls and the floor
- Buy tiles, floors, decorations and ever better buckets
- From broken loo to luxury bathroom

Everything stays on your own device: no account, no ads, no tracking. Playable offline.

Made by Studio Sedillo.
